Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy describes how Virmo handles information when you use its pet health diary.

01

Information you choose to save

Virmo stores pet names, species, breed, birthdays, sex, microchip information, weight, care dates, medication instructions, symptom observations, expenses, photos and audio voice notes that you choose to enter. This information is stored in the application storage on your device.

02

Device permissions

Camera permission is used to take pet and medical record photos. Photo library permission is used to select images for a pet or record. Microphone permission is used to record a voice note. Notification permission is used for local care reminders. Permission is requested through the system. You can change access in device Settings and continue using text records without camera, photo or microphone access.

03

Local storage and backups

Virmo does not operate a remote account or upload your pet records to an application server. Your device may include application data in a backup depending on your Apple settings. Exported archives contain the records and media you include, so keep those files in a place you trust. You control any copies stored outside Virmo.

04

Sharing and third-party services

Virmo does not send your health records, photos or voice notes to third-party analysis services. If you choose to share a PDF or archive, the destination you select receives that file with your permission. Apple processes purchases through the App Store under its own policies. Remote privacy and terms pages are retrieved from the configured website, which may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address.

05

Purchases

Verified App Store transaction identifiers and the remaining PDF export balance are stored locally to deliver purchased exports and prevent duplicate delivery. Virmo does not receive your full payment card information. Purchase processing and refund decisions are handled by Apple.

06

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them or erase local data. Deleting a health record removes its linked media from application storage. Erasing pet data retains the purchase ledger and remaining export balance to preserve purchased items. Removing the application may remove local data. Copies you previously shared or backed up must be managed at their destination.

07

Security and choices

Virmo uses the device application sandbox and file protection for saved records and media. No storage method guarantees complete security. Protect your device and backups. You may edit or delete saved records, export a health archive, and manage permissions in Settings.

08

Children and changes

Virmo is intended for pet caregivers and does not intentionally collect information from children through a remote service. This policy may change when app behavior changes. The effective date identifies the current text.

09

Contact

Questions about this policy may be addressed to virmo.petcare.inquiries@gmail.com.
